How I work

01Build to think

A clickable prototype ends a debate faster than a ten-page doc. I build the first version — an app, an agent, an explainer — so the team reacts to something real, then I write the spec around what we learned.

02Make AI measurable

Most AI features fail quietly. I set up evals before launch, audit them after, and design the fallbacks: what the model may do, what a human approves, and what we never let it touch.

03Tie the product to the P&L

Every roadmap item answers who pays, why now and how we'll know. I've owned pricing engines, ARR lines and partner channels, so revenue is part of the design, not an afterthought.

04Explain it so it moves

Interactive explainers, short films and one-page specs. If a leader, a seller and an engineer can all retell the idea after one read, it ships.
