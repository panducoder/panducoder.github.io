How I found my way in

Origins · 2010–2016

Engineer first

I started as an engineer because I liked knowing how things work. Writing data pipelines for Wall Street taught me the first rule I still use: if people depend on it every morning, it isn't done until it's boring.

Going places · 2016–2021

From code to customers

Then I went to sell. Pitching IoT to factory owners taught me that nobody buys a protocol — they buy what stops breaking.

My first 0-to-1 product taught me the other half: when there is no data, the first job is deciding what to trust.

The turning point · 2021–2026

AI, for real customers

Building AI for real customers changed how I think about product. Demos are easy. Trust is the product: grounding, evals, a human in the loop where it matters, and a price the customer can defend.
