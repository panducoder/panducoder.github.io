AI role-play, held to account by evals

At MindTickle: LLM buyers that sellers practise on — and the eval framework that keeps them honest. A 40% lift.

2021–2026Shipped at scaleSenior PM, MindTickle
What the eval audit foundClick a cell; reveal the audit
01The problem

An LLM can play a buyer, but if it is too easy, off-script or grades unfairly, sellers stop trusting it — and practice that isn't trusted doesn't happen.

Quality had to be measured, not eyeballed.

02How it works
  1. Simulated buyers. LLM buyer personas for cold calls, negotiation and discovery.
  2. Scenario grounding. Each buyer is grounded in the customer's products, personas and sales method.
  3. Eval suites. Automated checks on every scenario type: staying in character, realistic objections, fair grading, valid output.
  4. Audit. Audited 20+ evals and found blind spots in schema compliance and input traceability.
  5. Remediate. A tiered remediation plan: fix what breaks trust first.
03Decisions & trade-offs
ChoseEvals per scenario type
OverOne generic quality score
WhyA good cold-call buyer and a good negotiation buyer fail in different ways.
ChoseTiered remediation
OverFixing every eval gap at once
WhyGaps that change a seller's score come before cosmetic ones.
04My role

Product manager for AI role-play; author of the eval framework (50+ pages) and the audit of 20+ evals.

05Result

A 40% lift for the AI role-play system, and an eval framework the team built on.
