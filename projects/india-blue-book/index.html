India Blue Book

At Mahindra First Choice Wheels: India's first algorithmic used-car price — 40+ parameters, served by API to banks, insurers and dealers.

01The problem

India's used-car market had no trusted reference price. Banks lending on cars, insurers settling claims and dealers buying stock each guessed — and there was no clean data to start from.

02How it works
  1. Gather the data. Transactions and listings brought together from a market with no shared data infrastructure.
  2. Model the price. An algorithm across 40+ parameters: make, model, variant, age, kilometres, city, condition and more.
  3. Serve by API. API-first, so banks, insurers and dealers plug the price into their own systems.
  4. Launch 0-to-1. Positioning, pricing and the first partners for a product category that didn't exist.
03Decisions & trade-offs
ChoseAPI-first for institutions
OverA consumer price-check website first
WhyBanks and insurers make decisions on thousands of cars and need the price inside their own workflow.
ChoseA price range
OverA single number
WhyCondition varies; a fair range is more honest and more useful for a loan or claim.

Product manager, from idea to launch.

05Result

Launched as India's first algorithmic used-car pricing engine, used by banks, insurers and dealerships.
