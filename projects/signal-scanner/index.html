01The problem

The best moment to offer AI assurance is when a company's AI goes into production and its first enterprise buyer starts asking questions. Paid intent-data tools are expensive and miss the technical signals.

02How it works
  1. Watchlist. A seed list of AI companies, with their pricing, trust, subprocessor, jobs and blog URLs discovered automatically.
  2. Detectors. New AI vendors on subprocessor lists, usage-based pricing, trust-page certifications, AI hiring, launches and discussion threads.
  3. Score. Snapshots in SQLite. A first sighting scores 40%; real changes score full strength, 1–9.
  4. Account score. The strongest signal per type over 30 days, summed.
  5. Claude triage. Every weekday a scheduled Claude task reviews strong signals, writes a why-now note, and only notifies for HOT or WATCH.
