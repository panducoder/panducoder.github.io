Grounding doc builder

A Claude Code plugin that turns a customer's sales files into a first-draft grounding document — with a Word comment on every gap.

01The problem

Every customer onboarding to AI role-play needed a grounding document: who they sell to, what, how, and what good looks like per role. Assembling it from scattered decks and playbooks took days.

02How it works
  1. Ingest. Confirm the customer and pilot roles, then read uploaded PDFs, Word, PowerPoint, Excel or Drive files.
  2. Map to four pillars. Content is mapped to WHO, WHAT, HOW and ROLES, with a coverage map: complete, partial or missing.
  3. Research the gaps. Fill gaps from the customer's website, job posts and review sites — always flagged for confirmation.
  4. Ask, don't guess. Targeted clarifying questions for what is still missing.
  5. Render the draft. A styled .docx with native Word margin comments and a prioritised reviewer punch-list.
03Decisions & trade-offs
ChoseComment on gaps instead of guessing
OverFilling every section
WhyA wrong grounding doc produces a wrong AI buyer; a flagged gap gets fixed.
04My role

I wrote the spec, the intake question bank and the research guide, and packaged it as a two-command plugin for the team.

05Result

Distributed internally as a v1.0 plugin.
