An AI coach that hands over the real thing

A sales coach that answers in the company's own method — and returns the approved worksheet, diagram or video, not just text.

● Building nowAI productsProduct lead · design, PRD, prototypes
Follow one question through the coachPick a request, then click the steps
01The problem

Managers can't watch or coach every seller. Generic AI assistants fill the gap with generic advice that ignores the method the company has trained its people on.

Worse, a coach that can only reply in text leaves the best material unused: approved planners, diagrams and videos sit in a file store nobody opens mid-deal.

02How it works
  1. Jobs research. Interviews and competitive study define what a coach is actually hired to do. Every job gets a stable ID that is traced into the requirements.
  2. Specialist agents. Several specialist agents reason on the server behind a single tool. Only the finished answer reaches the seller's own AI assistant.
  3. Governed library. A registry sits between the file store and the coach. A person approves every tag card, and the coach can only hand out approved assets.
  4. Fill, don't write. Filling a planner is mapping, not writing: every value traces back to agent output, deal context or the seller's own words.
  5. Exact visuals. Diagrams are drawn from brand templates with exact values. Image models are only used for illustrations with no text in them.
  6. Customer's own AI. A customer's AI can query the library over MCP. Content that is never ingested can never leak.
03Decisions & trade-offs
