A partner enablement platform

At MindTickle: onboarding, certifying and tracking channel partners across thousands of partner organisations — $2.5M ARR.

2021–2026Shipped at scaleSenior PM, MindTickle

01The problem

Companies that sell through channel partners can't see whether those partners are trained, certified or performing. Partner reps sit outside the company's own systems, and thousands of partner organisations each need their own view.

02How it works
  1. Onboard. Partner organisations and their reps get access without becoming employees in the customer's systems.
  2. Train. The same readiness content employees get, scoped per partner tier.
  3. Certify. Certification paths that partner programs can tie benefits to.
  4. Track. Performance tracking per partner organisation, rolled up for the channel team.
03Decisions & trade-offs
ChosePartner organisations as first-class accounts
OverTreating partner reps as extra employee seats
WhyChannel teams manage partners as companies, with their own admins, tiers and reporting.
ChoseCertification as the core loop
OverContent access alone
WhyCertification is what partner programs reward, so it's what partners actually complete.
