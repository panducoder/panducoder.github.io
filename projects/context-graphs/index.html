Context graphs, explained

An interactive explainer on the missing layer of enterprise AI: not what is true, but why it became true.

01The problem

Systems of record store what is true now — ‘deal closed at $50K’. They don't store why: the exception, the approval, the Slack thread. AI agents have no precedent to reason from.

02How it works
  1. Two clocks. A state clock (what's true now) versus an event clock (decisions, exceptions, approvals over time).
  2. Versus knowledge graphs. Context graphs are verb-centric, temporal and built to fit an agent's context window.
  3. Informed walkers. Agents traverse the graph to find precedent before they act.
  4. Applied. Worked through applications by domain, including sales readiness.
