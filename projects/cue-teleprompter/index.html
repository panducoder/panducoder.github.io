Cue: a teleprompter that listens

A native Mac teleprompter that floats under the camera, follows your voice word by word and waits when you stop.

2026Tools & appsSolo build
Try it · Read along
Read alongTry stopping, then say take
01The problem

Normal teleprompters scroll at a fixed speed. You drift, retakes mean re-scrolling, and your eyes leave the lens.

02How it works
  1. Import a script. Markdown with sections, cues, emphasis and hidden notes — or txt, docx, rtf.
  2. Sit under the camera. A slim panel just under the webcam, hidden from screen recordings.
  3. Follow the voice. Speech recognition tracks where you are, jumps back on retakes, pauses on silence and resumes when you say ‘take’.
  4. Hotkeys everywhere. Play, sections and text size work inside Zoom, QuickTime or OBS.
03My role

Built in Swift with self-tests; it's the prompter my video pipeline loads scripts into.

04Result

Working app; my video pipeline loads its scripts into it.
