Redesigning an admin console for AI agents

Four studies and a clickable prototype: what if a support admin console were organised around outcomes, not settings?

2026Product designSelf-directed product studyPrototype source ↗
The same console, reorganisedSwitch views
01The problem

Support admin consoles are organised around the vendor's feature catalog — triggers, SLAs, brands. As AI agents take over resolution, admins need to see whether the AI is working and why it hands off, not hunt through settings.

02How it works
  1. Platform audit. Walked every admin screen in a trial account and mapped what exists.
  2. Jobs to be done. Two kinds of jobs: operating a business-critical platform, and running a support operation. Eleven job families in total.
  3. Navigation study. Re-grouped the console around how admins think: operate, improve, control.
  4. End-state prototype. A clickable control tower: escalation reasons, top failing intents, knowledge gaps tied to failed resolutions, and a trace of how one ticket was solved.
03Decisions & trade-offs
ChoseOrganise by outcome (operate, resolve, improve, control)
OverThe vendor's feature catalog
WhyAdmins are hired to keep support working, not to manage triggers.
04My role

Everything: the research, the four interactive studies and the single-file prototype.

05Result

A complete design argument, from first principles to a clickable end state.
