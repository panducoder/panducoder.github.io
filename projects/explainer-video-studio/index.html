01The problem

My first videos barely reached anyone: one boosted video got 9,000 impressions but a 9-second average watch. The first seconds didn't hook someone scrolling with the sound off.

02How it works
  1. Script. A script skill asks its questions once, researches, writes hook and beats, and loads the script into my teleprompter.
  2. Record and clean. Retakes cut, voice cleaned to −14 LUFS, a music bed generated, captions force-aligned.
  3. Muted-first plan. Frame 0 is the most surprising real visual with the stake burned in; nothing changes faster than about two seconds.
  4. Build scenes. Motion graphics built in After Effects by script, or with my own Python renderer for fast cuts.
  5. QC and ship. Automated overlap checks at 0, 1 and 3 seconds at phone size; max two renders; cover and captions.
03My role

The skills, the After Effects kit, the renderer and about 30 videos.

04Result

Ongoing, with a measured growth plan and lessons per video.
