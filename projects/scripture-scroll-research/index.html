01The problem

A short-video format for scripture took off for Christian content. Would the same pattern work for the Bhagavad Gita — and what makes the onboarding convert?

02How it works
  1. Capture. Frame-by-frame capture of a 55-second onboarding recording.
  2. Public data. App store charts, reviews and ads for the US and India.
  3. Annotate. Every onboarding and paywall screen annotated for what it is doing.
  4. Triangulate. Revenue estimated from several third-party estimators.
  5. Survey. About 360 Gita and Hindu apps ranked by rating, price and complaints.
03My role

Two research documents and a verse dataset for a prototype.

04Result

The pattern is proven elsewhere; Gita versions are new and weak. Deciding whether to build.
