OpenStage

A self-hostable, open-source tool for reviewing and approving creative work — pins, versions and step-by-step sign-off.

2026Tools & appsSolo build · AGPL-3.0
Review a fileDrop pins, cycle approvals
01The problem

Feedback on creative work is scattered across email, chat and spreadsheets, and nobody knows who still has to approve.

02How it works
  1. Upload versions. Images, PDFs and video, with numbered versions per file.
  2. Define steps. Ordered review steps with internal members, or external reviewers invited by a no-login link.
  3. Annotate. Pins, boxes, freehand, PDF highlights and timestamped video comments.
  4. Roll up. A status engine rolls decisions from reviewer to step to file.
  5. Notify. In-app and email, with pluggable storage and mail adapters.
03My role

The whole MVP, plus architecture, data-model and self-hosting docs.

04Result

MVP feature set complete; roadmap for audio, website review and version compare.
