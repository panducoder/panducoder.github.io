Self-serve AI sales coaching

At MindTickle: observe what a seller does, diagnose the gap with three agents, and generate the exact role-play they need next.

2026AI productsSenior PM, MindTickle
What should this seller practise next?Pick a seller
01The problem

How do you give a seller the best human coach when there is no human coach? Rich diagnostics already existed, but they were silent — nobody turned them into the next thing to practise.

02How it works
  1. Collect signals. Call AI, role-play reviews and assessment scores per rep, batched weekly, only when there are at least two signals.
  2. Three-agent diagnosis. A diagnosis agent finds strengths and gaps with verbatim evidence; a synthesis agent writes the narrative and urgency; an implication agent decides learn, practise or do.
  3. Auto-create the role-play. Six frameworks × the customer's grounding (who, what, how, roles) × the gap produce a just-in-time scenario.
  4. Recommendation waterfall. Critical gaps first, then skills decaying for 30+ days, then the role's baseline for new reps.
  5. Seller experience. A next-best-action banner, a skill-health radar, manager analytics and eval suites behind it all.
03Decisions & trade-offs
ChoseWait for two or more signals
OverRecommending from a single call
WhyOne bad call isn't a skill gap; acting on noise kills trust in the coach.

I wrote the strategy and PRD, the agent prompts and eval suites, the grounding questionnaires and the prompt-to-UI maps, and worked with design and engineering through build.
