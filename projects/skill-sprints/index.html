13-week skill sprints

Replacing one-day workshops with focused sprints that keep a skill alive — and measure it on real calls.

2026StrategyProduct design · launch film

Current work for my employer: names, customers, numbers and screens are left out. The visuals show the idea, not the product.

01The problem

Training is usually a moment: a workshop, then nothing. Skills fade within weeks, and at renewal the only proof is attendance — not behaviour change or quota impact.

02How it works
  1. Pick one focus. The client picks one skill per 13-week sprint, instead of trying to fix everything.
  2. Five stages. Each sprint moves through activate, prime, realize, brief and a day-45 follow-up.
  3. Signals from calls. A seller-signals lab scores reps on the focus skill from their real calls.
  4. Needs-you list. Managers get a short list of who needs them this week, with the evidence.
  5. Explain it. An explainer, a deck and a launch film built frame by frame.
03My role

I designed the program and the seller-signals prototype, and produced the explainer and launch film.

04Result

Prototype, deck, explainer and launch film complete.
