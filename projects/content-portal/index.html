A portal for licensed training content

Self-service access to licensed content, with one deny-by-default rule deciding who sees what.

01The problem

Customer learning admins needed a governed, self-service way to find, track changes to and download the content they license — with access rules strict enough that each customer only ever sees their own material.

02How it works
  1. Business case. Restructured the investment case around AI priorities for the coming year.
  2. Eleven requirements. Phase 1 is deliberately small: sign-in, entitled folders, change visibility, downloads, admin grants and uploads.
  3. One source of truth. One database of record for companies, contracts, products, entitlements, users and files.
  4. One access function. Every request goes through a single ‘is this allowed?’ function that says no unless it can prove yes.
  5. Usage to the warehouse. Every view and download becomes an event, so renewals can show real usage.
