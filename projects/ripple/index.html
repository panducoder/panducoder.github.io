Ripple: catch contradictions when one fact changes

Change ‘2-day workshop’ to ‘1-day’ and every sentence that no longer agrees lights up — with a fix to approve.

2026AI productsBuilder · internal fork
Try it · Try it
Try itChange the fact, then apply or keep fixes
01The problem

Change one fact in marketing or training content — a program name, a statistic, a workshop length — and contradictions hide elsewhere in the document. Proofreading misses them.

02How it works
  1. Watch the edit. A browser extension captures the text before a change. About two seconds after you stop typing, it sends before, after and the other sentences to a service.
  2. Classify, don't write. A classifier that cannot write labels each sentence: likely conflict, worth reviewing, or unaffected. It only flags at 80% confidence or more.
  3. Suggest a fix. A small, fast model proposes replace, delete, keep or ‘needs info’, using editor rules and optional house facts.
  4. Human decides. You apply, remove or keep each one. Nothing changes on its own.
03Decisions & trade-offs

OverOne LLM that finds and fixes in one go
WhyDetection stays predictable, and only flagged sentences get a suggested fix.
