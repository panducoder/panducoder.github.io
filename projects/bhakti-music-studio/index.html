01The problem

A first AI generation is not a finished song. Making Hindi and Bengali devotional music that sounds human takes directed lyrics, repairs and mixing — while watching the credit spend.

02How it works
  1. Brief and lyrics. Language, deity, modern feeling and emotional arc; lyrics in a pure register, approved word for word before any generation.
  2. Generate. Takes through Suno with a style prompt and a credit cap.
  3. Measure. Python tools for loudness, stereo width, spectrogram and lyric alignment, with a postmortem per take.
  4. Direct the stems. MIDI for melody, locally processed stems for the mix: quiet opening, space in the bridge, choir only in choruses.
  5. Master and learn. A local master, at least two measured passes, then lessons logged for the next song.
03My role

The workflow, the analysis tools and the songs.

04Result

Five songs documented so far, in Hindi and Bengali; one a day is the goal.
