Rebuilding a skills assessment with AI role-play

Explaining how a sales skills assessment really scores people — and designing its next version on AI role-play.

● Building nowAI productsProduct lead · analysis, roadmap, prototype
Try it · Why 3 out of 5 isn't one score

01The problem

Customers struggled to activate a simulation-based sales skills assessment and to read its reports. Few people could explain how a score was produced, and results weren't connected to pipeline or revenue.

The business needed to understand it end to end — and design a better next version.

02How it works
  1. Understand today. Analysed the existing assessment's documentation, outputs, sample reports and UX.
  2. Explain the score. An LLM grades each moment pass or not; grades pool per capability; an item-response model estimates skill; the result is rescaled and labelled. I recreated a cohort report with a script to check the explanation.
  3. Connect to revenue. Dashboards and deal reviews tie assessment use to pipeline, so the roadmap is argued with commercial data.
  4. Rebuild plan. Generate role-plays and questions from each customer's own material, re-grade every transcript and keep a per-seller skill profile. Start with a three-week manual pilot.
  5. Working prototype. A Next.js proof of concept built by coordinated AI agents against frozen contracts. Learner APIs never expose rubrics, and a fake-AI mode runs it with no keys.
03Decisions & trade-offs
ChoseStart with a three-week manual pilot
OverBuilding the full platform first
WhyProve customers value the new report before investing in automation.
ChoseNever expose rubrics to learner APIs
OverOne API for everyone
WhyIf learners can see the rubric, the assessment stops measuring skill.
04My role

I wrote the current-state dossier, the scoring explainer and the Now/Next/Later roadmap, designed the screens, and set up the agent-built proof of concept with its contracts and decision log.

05Result

A current-state dossier, a scoring explainer, a Now/Next/Later roadmap and a phased plan whose first step is a three-week manual pilot. The proof of concept is scaffolded with its contracts and decision log.
